Principles

How we build every app.

These aren't features we add later. They're the rules each app starts with.

Private by design

What you enter stays on your device. We don't run servers that collect it, and we never sell data.

Calm, not pushy

No ads, no dark patterns, no guilt-trip streaks. Apps that help, then get out of your way.

Works offline

No account and no sign-in. Open the app and it just works, wherever you are.

Made for everyone

Many languages, light and dark modes, and support for large text from day one.

The studio

Small on purpose.

Cykie Labs is an independent studio. We have no investors pushing for growth at any cost, so we can make apps the way we'd want them made for ourselves.

We'd rather ship a few apps people love and trust than many they forget. Each one is designed, built and supported by the people who made it.

  1. Start with a real need

    Every app begins with an everyday problem worth solving well.

  2. Keep it small and private

    Only the features that matter, with your data kept on your device.

  3. Care for it long-term

    Regular updates, real support and listening to the people who use it.
