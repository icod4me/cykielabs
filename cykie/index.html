Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone, in Cykie's private storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, so the developer can't see it either.

How do I move to a new phone?

In Cykie, open Settings › Your data › Export to save a backup file, then use Import on the new phone.

Can Cykie be used as contraception?

No. Its predictions are estimates from your logged cycles and aren't a reliable way to prevent pregnancy.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start — there's nothing to sign up for.

Is it on Android?

Cykie is on the App Store for iPhone now. Android is on the way.
