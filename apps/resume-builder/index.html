Resume Builder

A clean, ATS friendly CV in minutes, made entirely on your iPhone.

Fill in your details once, try them in 26 templates and export a polished PDF in seconds. The whole app, PDF included, runs on your phone, so your work history never sits on someone else's server.

Features

What's inside.

26 real templates

Classic, two column, timeline, minimal, academic and more. Not one layout in different colours.

Step by step

A guided form covers experience, education, skills, projects, languages and references.

Print quality PDF

Your email, phone, LinkedIn and GitHub become tappable links. Share, print or save to Files.

One CV per job

Duplicate a CV, tweak it for a role and export it in another template whenever you like.

Your look

Light and dark themes and six accent colours. Add a profile photo once and reuse it.

Works offline

No sign up and no internet needed. Delete all your data with one tap.

Privacy

No account and no server. Everything you type stays on your device, and you can wipe it all with one tap.
