Live wallpapers

Skyshift

Wallpapers that keep time with you, drawn live in code.

Mornings look like morning, sunsets turn gold, and after dark the stars, city windows and northern lights come on. Every scene is drawn in code, so there are no image packs to download.

Features

What's inside.

167 living scenes

Nature, cities, night skies, safari, fantasy and more, animated at up to 60 fps.

Follows your clock

Kites by day, meteors by night. Scrub through any hour or watch a whole day as a time lapse.

Surprise Me

Tap once and Skyshift grows a universe nobody has seen before, with its own name.

Set it as your wallpaper

Save a crisp still, or a Live Photo that moves when you press your Lock Screen.

A living clock

A full screen clock over the sky, clock faces and a live moon phase card.

Skyshift Plus

An optional subscription that adds Scene Studio, Live Photos, extra clock faces and more.

Privacy

Fully offline. No ads, no analytics and no account. The only permission it asks for is saving photos you choose.
