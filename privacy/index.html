Privacy

Website privacy

This page covers cykielabs.com. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked below.

What this website collects

Nothing on purpose. The site has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking scripts, and it loads no fonts or code from other companies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep short lived technical logs such as IP addresses to run and protect the service. We don't receive or use those logs.

When you email us

If you write to hello@cykielabs.com, we use your address and message only to reply and help you. We don't add you to a mailing list and we don't share your details.

Our apps

  • Cykie privacy policy
  • Skyshift privacy policy
  • Resume Builder: no account and no server. What you enter stays on your device. Details are on its App Store page.
